About Me

Hello! I'm Sumanth Kota. I'm a person who loves learning how things work. As a child I was always interested in the inner workings of machines, trying to understand every detail. I believe that is why I chose to become an electrical engineer, it involved everything that I was interested in. During my undergrad, I gained an interest on machine learning and neural networks, and I slowly taught myself. Back then, LLMs hadn't become the powerhouses they are today. In fact the 'Attention Is All You Need' paper had been only recently released, but those subjects were the starting point for my interest in data science, machine learning and neural networks. There are also a lot of similarities between subjects in electrical engineering and data science, which made the transition into what I was now engrossed in more smooth. After joining my masters in Data Science, I was learning even more about the mathematics of machine learning, and during my masters, I had found what fascinated me the most, which is analyzing neural networks, and that comes to the bigger picture of creating Artifical General Intelligence (AGI). AGI is what many neural network researchers and scientists are working on right now, and I have also a curiosity towards it. I do not know if inspiration from the human brain will be a necessity for AGI, or whether just some additional components to the architectures we have now will cause it to emerge, but I am also delighted towards the inner working of the human brain. Since taking those few subjects during my undergrad, to now, neural networks have evolved exponentially. There are tons of new architectures, and more people are taking a notice towards them; in my free time I'm always reading about new advances in neural networks. I have a hunch AGI might be a possiblity in my lifetime, and I hope to be a part of that discovery.

What I'm working researching now: Noise and Adversarial Attack Robustness on Neural Networks, Mechanistic Interpretability
